# Section a. Gold-nanoparticle engineering (draft v0.2, 2026-11-05)

Status: statements are **abstract only** unless marked **[FULL TEXT]**. Six papers were read in full via PubMed Central open access (R04212, R07685, R04815, R07002, R05311, R06317); four key papers (R08618, R06544, R05924, R06939) are not in PMC and still need PDFs. LOD values are as stated in abstracts, in each paper's own units, matrix and comparator; they are not comparable across studies. 97 included records (93 from 2015-2026); the ones discussed are listed here and every one must appear in the main-text table.

## Questions this section answers
1. Which AuNP properties (size, shape, structure, conjugation) were varied, and how much did the paper's own LOD change?
2. Which on-strip enhancement steps keep a colorimetric (visible) readout?
3. What recognition elements replace conventional IgG on the particle?
4. How are papers with plain colloidal-gold strips and no engineering handled? (Grouped at the end.)

## 1. Particle size, conjugation and surface chemistry
- Particle diameter changed the potato virus X LOD: 80 to 3 ng/mL across 6.4-33.4 nm particles and 9 ng/mL at 52 nm (DOI: 10.1007/s00216-012-5985-8). Pre-2015; plant virus; leaf extract.
- Size-controlled AuNPs (34.2-137.8 nm) were used for HBsAg (DOI: 10.3390/s16122154); LOD not reported in the abstract. An 80 nm AuNP was used in a dengue NS1 test with serotype-specific LODs of 2.9, 0.5, 25.2 and 4.5 ng/mL (DENV-1 to -4) on 185 archived plasma samples (DOI: 10.3390/diagnostics16030395).
- Conjugation chemistry: hydrazide-assisted directional conjugation of HBsAg antibody to AuNPs reduced the labelled antibody needed (0.2 mg/pmol AuNP); LOD not reported (DOI: 10.1016/j.aca.2021.338623). EDTA-K2 improved SARS-CoV-2 IgM/IgG sensitivity, no values (DOI: 10.2147/ijn.s281594). Protein corona and probe aggregation state influenced dengue and Zika NS1 assay performance (DOI: 10.1021/acs.bioconjchem.6b00523; DOI: 10.3390/pharmaceutics14112439; DOI: 10.1021/acsami.0c08628).

## 2. On-strip enlargement and enhancement (colour retained)
- **[FULL TEXT]** In situ enlargement of AuNPs for SARS-CoV-2 RBD, same antibodies and same 22.8 nm AuNPs in all four formats, RBD spiked into saliva from one healthy volunteer: LOD 488 pg/mL (conventional), 61 (silver enhancement), 8 (galvanic-assisted Au deposition, the authors' new step), 1 pg/mL (gold enhancement). LODs were computed as blank mean + 3 SD from office-scanner grayscale images, so they are instrument-read, not naked-eye values. Galvanic and gold enhancement gave nonspecific test-zone colouring; gold enhancement was visible by eye only over a narrower dilution range of inactivated virus. Gold enhancement detected inactivated virus at a 500-fold higher dilution than the conventional strip; no patient samples were tested (DOI: 10.3390/bios11070229).
- Post-assay growth of 20 nm gold to about 350 nm gave a 240-fold lower LOD for potato virus X than conventional LFIA (DOI: 10.1007/s00604-018-3052-7).
- Self-enhancement for SARS-CoV-2 N protein in saliva: 0.50 ng/mL by naked eye, 0.10 ng/mL with calibration, 10x conventional (DOI: 10.1016/j.snb.2023.133898).
- Gold nanoflowers (135 nm) with gold staining: 25 pg/mL; nanoflowers were 5x more sensitive than same-size AuNPs (DOI: 10.1007/s00604-024-06502-1).
- Electrophoresis-assisted multilayer assembly (biotin-streptavidin) raised HBV sensitivity from 73% to 98% in 118 clinical samples, with specificity unchanged at 95% (DOI: 10.1002/anie.202215548). Reference method not stated in the abstract.

## 3. Alternative plasmonic structures
- **[FULL TEXT]** Plasmon colour-preserved (PLASCOP) AuNP clusters, streptavidin core with biotinylated-antibody satellites keeping about 15 nm gaps so the red colour is preserved: LOD for SARS-CoV-2 N protein by ChemiDoc imaging (S/N = 5) 0.910 (15 nm AuNP), 0.226 (40 nm AuNP), 0.551 (40-15 cluster) and 0.038 ng/mL (40-40 cluster); the 40-15 cluster was worse than single 40 nm particles. In saliva spiked with virus (12-355 TCID50/mL) LOD 54 TCID50/mL. The "clinical feasibility" test is spiked saliva only, no patient samples (DOI: 10.1016/j.bios.2022.114094).
- SiO2@Au core-satellite particles: 0.24 pg/mL, two orders of magnitude below colloidal gold (DOI: 10.1021/acsami.2c13303); readout visual/reader.
- Gold nanoparticle dimers (Ag-ion soldering) for porcine epidemic diarrhea virus: 2.0 x 10^2 TCID50/mL, 50x a commercial colloidal-gold strip; 109 clinical samples; good agreement with RT-PCR (DOI: 10.1007/s00604-023-06013-5).
- Ag-Au nanoshells for bovine viral diarrhea virus: 1.95e3 TCID50/mL, 4x the AuNP LFIA; 215 clinical samples; 98.14% concordance with ELISA (DOI: 10.1007/s00604-026-08241-x).
- Au/Fe3O4 core-shell particles for avian influenza H7: 10^3.5 EID50; 7 positives in 200 clinical samples (DOI: 10.1039/c8ra00185e).
- **[FULL TEXT]** Plasmonic scattering for influenza A (DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-58663-z): a refractive-index-matching step makes the nitrocellulose transparent over a black backing card, so 100 nm AuNPs appear as orange scattering lines on a black background. LOD 2.09 pfu/mL (H1N1 particles) vs 755.47 pfu/mL for a conventional 40 nm AuNP strip (361-fold) and 5,417-9,219 pfu/mL for three commercial strips (stated as 2600-4400x); N protein 1.17 vs 234.69 pg/mL (201-fold). Clinical: 30 qRT-PCR-positive influenza A swabs and 12 healthy controls, sensitivity 90% (27/30), specificity 100%; commercial strips 23-30%, conventional strip 40%. The samples were diluted to 10% in buffer, which the authors acknowledge disadvantages the commercial strips. **Boundary case:** the authors state the signal is scattering, not absorbance (colorimetric); it is visible by eye with extra light, read by Chemi-doc and smartphone. Needs a decision on whether it counts as colorimetric (see below).
- Magnetic-gold nanostars for Rift Valley fever virus nucleoprotein: 2.24 pg/mL, 1000x vs GNP assays, in PBS only (DOI: 10.1021/acsami.5c21544). Matrix is buffer, not clinical.
- Two-wavelength imaging of composite nanoparticles for HIV capsid protein: 170 fM, 58-fold over single-wavelength 50 nm AuNP (DOI: 10.1016/j.bios.2022.114133). **Flag:** instrument imaging, not naked eye.

## 4. Replacing the antibody on the particle
- **[FULL TEXT]** Nanobody-based dual-gold strip for SARS-CoV-2 S1: AuNP-nanobody (or AuNP-mAb) detector with AuNP-ACE2 as the test-line capture probe; 40 nm AuNPs; gel-documentation intensity readout. LOD for gamma-irradiated virus is 4-fold lower with the nanobody (0.4 vs 1.6, x 10^n copies/mL; the exponent is lost in the retrieved text, verify in the PDF). Saliva from 320 symptomatic people (180 RT-PCR positive, 140 negative): at Ct <= 30 nanobody sensitivity 97.14% and specificity 98.57%. **Internal inconsistency:** the abstract gives mAb sensitivity 90.04% at Ct <= 30, the results text gives 83.33%; check the tables in the PDF (DOI: 10.1038/s41598-023-37347-y). Other nanobody papers (abstract data only): DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.4c00509; DOI: 10.1039/d4nr02762k; DOI: 10.1039/d5nr02568k; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2025.1707358; DOI: 10.3390/bios16020132.
- Aptamers: SARS-CoV-2 spike N-terminal-domain aptamers, 5 x 10^5 copies/mL, UV-inactivated virus (DOI: 10.1002/anie.202107730); AptaFlow, 10^6 copies/mL (DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.2c00554); **[FULL TEXT]** aptamer strip for spike protein on cold-chain packaging (DOI: 10.3390/molecules29081776): a competitive format, so the test line gets weaker as antigen increases; LOD 91.2 ng/mL (0.68 nM, 3 sigma/slope) with a Hamamatsu reader, visual range 0.1-1 ug/mL; recoveries 83.3-108.8% (RSD 2.3-6.2%) in thawed package-ice solutions spiked with recombinant S protein. No virus or patient samples were tested, and the authors state the sensitivity is lower than other methods.
- **[FULL TEXT]** Two-component amplifier for antibody (serology) detection: SARS-CoV-2 N protein on gold nanoparticles mixed with anti-N IgG on gold in a 5:1 ratio gave more than 2-fold higher test-line signal than N-protein-gold alone (DOI: 10.1002/gch2.202200008). The text describes 150 nm carboxyl gold nanoshells; the abstract says gold nanoparticles. LOD is expressed as ELISA optical density (visual 0.55 IgM, 0.4 IgG, equal to the ELISA cut-off), not a concentration. Against the in-house ELISA, sensitivity IgM 94% (103/109), IgG 96% (107/112); specificity IgM 93% (51/55), IgG 98% (51/52); 112 RT-PCR-positive and 52 pre-pandemic sera, 2 uL serum, smartphone and ImageJ readout.
- Other recognition elements: sialic-acid glyconanoparticles (DOI: 10.1021/acscentsci.0c00855), lectin (DOI: 10.1080/15321819.2023.2260480), miniprotein binder (DOI: 10.1007/s00604-023-06113-2).

## 5. Plain AuNP strips without engineering (grouped; details in table)
Many records describe new colloidal-gold strips for SARS-CoV-2 antigen/antibody, norovirus, SFTSV, HIV p24, varicella-zoster, monkeypox A29 (50 pg/mL purified A29; no cross-reaction with vaccinia or cowpox, DOI: 10.1039/d2an02043b) and animal viruses (canine parvovirus, PCV2, pseudorabies, avian leukosis). These are reported in the table, not discussed individually, unless full text shows a methodological contribution.

## Decisions needed
1. R07685 (plasmonic scattering): count as colorimetric? Authors position it as a signal type beyond absorbance (colorimetric), but it is a visible-light, naked-eye readout. Recommendation: keep it as a flagged boundary case in section a, with its LOD kept separate from absorbance-based strips.
2. R04212 and R04815: LODs are instrument-derived (scanner, ChemiDoc), not visual. Recommendation: report the readout beside each LOD.

## Gaps and cautions
- Most abstracts do not give matrix, n or reference method; those cells stay "not reported" until full text is read.
- Enhancement papers use different comparators (conventional strip, commercial strip, same-size AuNP), so fold-improvements are within-paper only.
- Several veterinary/plant entries are also covered in section i; each paper is assigned to one section only.
