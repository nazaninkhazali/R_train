# Section b. Other coloured labels (draft v0.1, 2026-11-05)

Status: statements are **abstract only** unless marked **[FULL TEXT]**. Four key papers were read in full via PubMed Central open access (R06083, R08280, R05403, R04517). Four key papers still need PDFs: R05096, R06199, R02878, R03310. 38 included records (34 from 2015-2026). LODs are in each paper's own units, matrix and comparator; no cross-study ranking. Readout (visual, camera/ImageJ, reader) is stated where known.

## Questions this section answers
1. Which non-gold colour carriers have been used (latex, carbon, selenium, silver, polymer and dye-based, cellulose nanobeads, magnetic)?
2. For each, what did the same paper's own comparator show (gold strip, commercial strip, fluorescent label)?
3. Where does a colour carrier give something gold cannot (multicolour multiplexing)?
4. Where do colour labels fall short (fluorescent comparisons)?

## 1. Latex microspheres (single colour)
- **[FULL TEXT]** Red latex microspheres for porcine circovirus 2 (PCV2) antigen (DOI: 10.1007/s00253-022-12074-y): mAb 7C7 on red latex, mAb 9H4 as capture; minimum detection amounts recorded as 10^2.5 (PCV2a), 10^0.7 (PCV2b) and 10^1.5 (PCV2d) TCID50/0.1 mL, with the strip clearly less sensitive for PCV2a. No cross-reaction with 10 other porcine viruses. Against PCR on 100 tissue and anal-swab samples (53 PCR-positive, 47 negative): sensitivity 81.13% (43/53), specificity 100% (47/47), agreement 90.00%, kappa 0.720; 10 PCR-positive samples were missed. The authors attribute this partly to PCR detecting viral DNA that is not infectious virus. Positive rate of 22.95% in 183 field samples is surveillance, not accuracy. No gold-strip comparator in this paper.
- **[FULL TEXT]** Dyed latex beads (about 300 nm) for porcine epidemic diarrhea virus (PEDV) in faeces, with an added filter pad between sample and conjugate pads (DOI: 10.1186/s44149-021-00029-1): LOD 10^3.60 TCID50/mL as recorded from the abstract chart (the exponent is lost in the retrieved text; verify in the PDF), detected 100% at that level in three strains. 108 faecal samples against RT-PCR (70 positive, 38 negative): 8 false negatives, no false positives, sensitivity 88.57%, specificity 100%, agreement 92.59%, kappa 0.845. The paper compares its 92.59% with 74.07% (gold) and 86.67% (fluorescent) taken from other studies; this is not a head-to-head comparison. Read by eye and by smartphone with ImageJ; storage stable 56 days at room temperature and 37 C, 23 days at 50 C (qualitative).
- Latex strips for other viruses (abstract data only): SARS-CoV-2 antigen 25 ng/mL (DOI: 10.1002/jcla.24091); North American PRRSV 5 x 10^2.0 TCID50/mL, sensitivity 90.32% (DOI: 10.3389/fmicb.2022.882112); SARS-CoV-2 N protein strip 10 ng/mL against 15 pg/mL for a sandwich ELISA (DOI: 10.1111/1751-7915.70117); a latex double-antigen sandwich for neutralizing antibodies, LOD 7.11 ng/mL, sensitivity 98.29% and specificity 97.70% against virus neutralization, in the same paper a fluorescent version reached 1.13 ng/mL (DOI: 10.1016/j.talanta.2022.124200).

## 2. Multicolour carriers for multiplexing
- **[FULL TEXT]** Red, blue and black latex microspheres for simultaneous African and classical swine fever virus antibodies (DOI: 10.1155/tbed/5512419): red line ASFV (p72), blue line CSFV (E2), black control. Visual LOD 1:256 for both antibodies against a commercial ELISA at 1:256 (ASFV) and 1:64 (CSFV), i.e. equal for ASFV and 4-fold better for CSFV, from one standard positive serum. 180 clinical sera against commercial ELISAs: ASFV 48 true positive, 1 false positive, 0 false negative, 131 true negative (kappa 0.986); CSFV 108 true positive, 5 false positive, 2 false negative, 65 true negative (sensitivity 98.2%, specificity 92.9%, kappa 0.918). No cross-reaction with six other pig viruses. Serum samples were diluted 10-fold; vaccination status and provenance of the sera are not described, and the authors state CSFV E2 antibodies cannot separate vaccinated from infected animals. **Correction to the abstract-level chart:** the 1:64 belongs to the CSFV ELISA, not to the strip.
- Other multicolour or dual-label work (abstract data only): ASFV antibodies with a two-colour latex strip, 1:1024, kappa 0.955, 159 samples (DOI: 10.1007/s00253-025-13694-w); bicolour latex for SARS-CoV-2 neutralizing antibodies, about 48 ng/mL, 13 sera (DOI: 10.3390/bios12020103); red/blue bicolour strip for norovirus GII and adenovirus F41, 5.8 and 63 copies/uL (DOI: 10.3389/fmicb.2026.1787049); multicolour nanobeads typing influenza A and B by colour, about 10x better than gold or latex, pre-2015 (DOI: 10.1016/j.jviromet.2014.08.025).

## 3. Carbon, selenium, silver and other inorganic labels
- Carbon black for severe fever with thrombocytopenia syndrome virus (SFTSV), 100 pg/mL, recoveries 91.58-105.4% in spiked serum (DOI: 10.1039/d3an00515a); carbon black in a vertical-flow format for SARS-CoV-2 IgG, LOD lower than colloidal gold (value not reported) (DOI: 10.3390/bios13090857).
- Gold vs carbon in one paper (porcine deltacoronavirus): 1:128 for gold and 1:64 for carbon, 137 faecal samples, agreement 92.7% (gold) and above 88% (carbon) (DOI: 10.1016/j.isci.2026.116948). Here the carbon label was less sensitive than gold.
- Selenium nanoparticles: SARS-CoV-2 IgM/IgG, 20 and 5 ng/mL, sensitivity 93.33%, specificity 97.34%, 90 COVID-19 sera and 263 controls (DOI: 10.1039/d0lc00828a); antigen strip with structured protein probes, 10 pg/mL, 192 samples, overall agreement 95.83% against RT-PCR (DOI: 10.1016/j.bios.2024.116878).
- Calixarene-stabilised silver nanoparticles for SARS-CoV-2 IgG: stated one order of magnitude lower than a gold LFIA, sensitivity 73%, specificity 100% (DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.2c00870).
- Ultramarine blue particles for HBsAg, 0.37 ng/mL (S/N = 3), better than a gold strip (DOI: 10.1016/j.aca.2019.11.037).
- Magnetic labels: Fe3O4@PMAA@IgG for SARS-CoV-2, 0.5 ng/mL (DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2026.150960); older work on Fe3O4@SiO2 for HBsAg, 5 pg/mL by eye and 0.1 pg/mL with a CCD reader, pre-2015 (DOI: 10.1166/jbn.2011.1352).

## 4. Polymer and dye-based labels
- **[FULL TEXT]** Polydopamine nanoparticles for SARS-CoV-2 IgG and IgM in a three-line strip (DOI: 10.3390/bios13030352): PDA core coated by polyelectrolyte layers, silica and PEG-silane to stop it sticking to nitrocellulose (230 nm growing to 320 nm); antigen and mouse IgG on the particle. LOD 1.51 ng/mL (IgM) and 2.34 ng/mL (IgG) from camera images analysed with ImageJ, in human serum spiked with standard antibodies (0-1000 ng/mL); no patient samples. In the same paper a commercial gold strip could barely detect 10-100 ng/mL IgM and IgG, but no fold-improvement is reported.
- Other organic or dye labels (abstract data only): polylevodopa nanoparticles for SARS-CoV-2 (5 pg/mL) and influenza A (0.1 ng/mL) (DOI: 10.1016/j.bbrc.2024.149821); dye-doped red silica for H7 avian influenza, 0.08 ng/mL (DOI: 10.1039/d1ay00204j); dye-loaded polymersomes (DOI: 10.1021/acssensors.1c00854); polydiacetylene with visible and fluorescent readouts, no values in the abstract (DOI: 10.1002/asia.201700769); disperse-dye IBDV antibody strip, pre-2015 (DOI: 10.1016/j.vetimm.2008.05.022).

## 5. Cellulose nanobeads
- SARS-CoV-2 antigen with scFv-Fc, 2 ng antigen and 2.5 x 10^4 pfu (DOI: 10.1016/j.bios.2020.112868); cellulose nanobeads vs AuNP for nucleocapsid antigen, cellulose cut-off 100-fold below the WHO target product profile threshold of 10^6 copies/mL while gold met it (DOI: 10.1039/d5ra08197a); Nipah virus nucleoprotein, 1 ng/mL, 4.1 x 10^7 copies/mL by reader and 8.3 x 10^7 by eye, inactivated culture only (DOI: 10.1080/22221751.2026.2716499).

## 6. Where colour labels were not the most sensitive
- Fluorescent microspheres were more sensitive than coloured microspheres for rotavirus/adenovirus (DOI: 10.1186/s12985-018-0951-5) and influenza A/B (DOI: 10.1186/s12985-019-1190-0); the fluorescent latex in the neutralizing-antibody paper beat the colorimetric latex (7.11 vs 1.13 ng/mL) (DOI: 10.1016/j.talanta.2022.124200); quantum dots reached 100% positive agreement against 96.9% for gold in an adenovirus-3 antibody strip, 90 sera (DOI: 10.3390/diagnostics16162670). These support a statement that colour carriers trade some sensitivity for equipment-free reading.

## Gaps and cautions
- Several abstracts give no sample number, matrix or reference method; those stay "not reported".
- Reference standards differ: PCR (PCV2, PEDV), ELISA (swine fever), virus neutralization, in-house standards. Agreement values are not interchangeable.
- Still needed as PDFs: R05096 (silver), R06199 (carbon black), R02878 (ultramarine blue), R03310 (selenium).
